| **D-023** | Fear is downstream of boredom; intervene at making | Boredom is *first* and fear is late, so treating fear directly treats a symptom. Mission one is *make something*, not *survive being seen*. | Accepted — chain extended by **D-027** |
| **D-024** | Present tense: cut every *will* and *have* mechanic | Countdowns, goal ceremonies and deadline projections (*will*), badges, streaks and portfolio-as-achievement (*have*) both pull the user out of now, which is where the product works. Retires `FR-017`. | Accepted |

| **D-026** | The artifact is a by-product, not the promise | Things you *have* devalue; a portfolio page is worth little in five years, and hours authored don't. The page stays as a receipt. | Accepted |
| **D-027** | Laziness is the chain's last link, and a verdict rather than a cause | Nobody says "I'm afraid to make a website"; they say "I couldn't be bothered". So laziness is what users **report** while fear is what **happens** — and it's the only link phrased as an identity, which pre-explains every future instance and makes this a stable loop rather than a bad week. Three consequences: the mirror's job is to make the verdict untenable against a record; interviews ask once more past it; and no copy may describe the user, only what they did. | Accepted |
| **D-028** | No scope, no finish line — never ask how big or by when | D-016 kept a user-set target date, which was inconsistent: D-024 had killed the projection while leaving the date that fed it. | **D-029** | Show, don't tell | A goal phrased as a feeling can't be built and can't be falsified, so goals are written as things the product *does*. | Accepted |
| **D-030** | Choice reveals preference; the recall questionnaire is retired | *"When did you last lose track of time?"* is redundant — people already know what absorbs them. Knowing was never the missing piece. A choice is evidence; a self-report is a performance. | Accepted |
| **D-031** | Session mood, before and after, is the one internal state recorded | A narrow exception to D-009, safe because of **who is the subject**: D-009 forbids the *product rating the user*; this is the *user rating a session*. It earns the exception because the delta is the only direct measure of the promise. | Accepted — see the open risk on how weak the number is |
| **D-032** | No account until there is something worth keeping | Registration before value gates the only thing that matters. **Cost:** anonymous sessions complicate storage, analytics, and the age gate. | Accepted |
| **D-033** | Crews deferred to *maybe* | A social component at signup is a barrier for exactly the person this targets — someone avoiding being seen. **Broke a dependency:** D-025's ladder used the crew as guaranteed first responder. | Accepted — but the replacement rung one was **wrong and is corrected by D-043**: this decision's own objection applies harder to a friend than to the crew it deferred |
| **D-034** | Delete *"how much time do you have?"* — every mission is small | A time budget is a deadline in miniature, asked every session instead of once, and it demands a plan from someone who has none: a bored person doesn't have "twenty minutes", they have an evening. Duration is **observed, not declared**. | Accepted |
| **D-035** | The session has to be enjoyable in itself, not only in its aftermath | A payoff that arrives only afterwards still loses the moment-to-moment fight against something engineered to feel good instantly. Every cheap route is already banned, which makes this a design problem rather than a feature request. | Accepted — supplied no mechanism for eleven decisions; **implemented by D-045** |
| **D-036** | Never ask what they want to improve — they don't know | Every self-improvement product opens by demanding the goal they don't have, which is where they stall. Direction emerges from what they keep choosing. **Caution:** "become more successful" is the user's motivation, not a promise we make. | Accepted |
| **D-037** | Platform reopened; mobile-first web withdrawn | The missions produce real artifacts and a beginner makes almost none of those on a phone; a five-minute phone mission would have to be trivial. **Recommendation: desktop web**, mobile at most a thin later surface — the trigger being on the phone doesn't mean the work has to be. | **Open** — decide before the first build story |
| **D-038** | Ask what they think they did, then show the record — comment on neither | Pointed at *discomfort* this mechanism was demoted as a late link; pointed at **achievement** it hits the terminal one, because the laziness verdict is a self-assessment and the cheapest way to dispute one is to set it beside the record. **Four rules:** words never numbers, since a numeric estimate invites gaming once people learn the record follows · show both and **narrate neither**, because commenting on the gap is the product describing the user · never fire against an empty record · only *after* work. | Accepted |
| **D-039** | Translate the record into words they can use — never into a claim about its value | Someone who shipped a small tool doesn't know how to say so; the missing thing is language, not worth. **Never assert value or predict how a reader takes it** — unfalsifiable, and where this category over-claims. **Never inflate.** | **Amended by D-041** |
| **D-040** | A third mission kind: **reset** — go outside, clear the table, walk away | *Clear the table* removes the reason you won't start; *go outside* is what you do after ninety minutes of staring. Framed as **conditions for making, never wellbeing** — the only thing standing between this and the tip-app genre. **Hard constraint: resets never count** toward authored hours, things made, or things shipped; padding the record with walks destroys the thing that disputes the verdict. **Anti-escape-hatch:** one per session, never a swap for a mission being avoided. | Accepted |
| **D-041** | Describe what they did in plain facts; never compose the sentence | D-039's generated personal-statement line was the product writing the user's application — D-005's failure mode in a different hat, unnoticed for a whole decision. It's also harmful: a paste-ready sentence means they never articulate it, and *"tell me about this"* then catches them with nothing. The gap this fills is **noticing**; phrasing is theirs, and it's the skill that survives into the room. | Accepted — supersedes the composed-sentence half of **D-039** |
| **D-042** | **Freedom, and consequence. Discipline is not a mechanism** | Discipline is force applied against what someone wants, and it fails the way all force fails — it needs a supply, and the supply runs out on a Tuesday. What replaces it, both halves load-bearing: **you are free to do whatever you want; you are not free of the consequences.** The first is why nothing is scheduled, owed, or streaked — a mechanic manufacturing obligation is lying to the user about their freedom, they notice, and they leave — and why the session is pleasant at all, since knowing you may walk at any second is what makes starting cheap. The second is why a record exists; freedom with no visible consequence is noise. **And the consequence shown is only ever the one they cannot already see.** The lost evening is not information — boredom is time you *feel* passing, so that consequence has already been delivered by the evening, and repeating it is scolding. Seven different days, a stranger using the thing: that's the only new fact available. Which reconciles consequence visibility with *never describe the user* — the mirror isn't half a record for being kind, it's the half that isn't already vivid. | Accepted — restates and supersedes **D-017** |
| **D-043** | The ladder is ordered by **number of recipients, not familiarity** | D-033 made rung one *one person the user already knows*, confusing *easy to reach* with *safe to fail in front of*. They run opposite here: a friend's judgment persists — they're at the table at Christmas, and a bad first thing becomes a thing about you — while a stranger's evaporates on contact. So the person the user knows was the **highest**-stakes audience, sitting at rung one. Rung one is now defined by one property, **a responder guaranteed to respond**, and the user picks who. **A stranger is the default offer.** | Accepted — amends **D-025** and the rung-one half of **D-033** |
| **D-044** | There is no search demand. **The complaint is the channel** | Demand §7 said distribution dominates and then said nothing — the largest hole in the planning, and load-bearing rather than an oversight: the obvious channel doesn't exist. Nobody searches for this, because the people furthest into the loop recognise it least as a problem, so intent-based acquisition has nothing to bid on. What they do is complain, publicly, in the chain's last-link language — and a complaint is the only reliable signal that someone is in the market. **Refused:** paid acquisition (LTV unknown, so no CAC is justifiable) and influencer content (the genre requires the promises we forbid). | Accepted |
| **D-045** | Enjoyment gets mechanisms, or D-035 was a wish | Five, all consequences of existing decisions. **The mission removes the decision, not the work** — what's aversive about starting is deciding, so a mission specific enough to begin with zero thinking has had the aversive part taken out. **The first move is mechanical** — a blank first minute is where sessions die. **Visible change under your hands** — a template only enters the library if a first session ends with something to look at. **The interface does nothing.** **Leaving is free**, because the cheapness of starting depends on it. Measured, not asserted, by unprompted continuation (`FR-068`). | Accepted — implements **D-035** |
| **D-046** | Phase 0 recruits **18+ only**. The 16–18 segment is blocked, not dropped | `FR-085` requires a named responsible adult per minor cohort and the founder is under 18, so **the founder cannot hold the role their own second segment depends on**. And `FR-080`–`FR-086` are *product* requirements, while the concierge phase collects personal data on forms with no product to gate — minors in Phase 0 would engage UK GDPR and a school's safeguarding policy with none of those safeguards attached to anything. **Cost:** closes the only zero-cost channel for the duration of the concierge test. **Unlock:** a named adult who will hold safeguarding, plus the DSL of any school involved. | Accepted — amends **D-019** |
| **D-047** | Separate the operator, the responder, and the interviewer | One person writing missions, guaranteeing the response, and running exit interviews stacks three demand characteristics on a cohort already unpaid, with participants reporting to whoever's personal reply they just received. Pre-registration stops us rationalising a number afterwards; it does nothing about one bent before it's written down. **Minimum: the exit interviewer is never the operator.** | Accepted — amends **D-018**, **D-020** |

## Open risks

| Risk | Why it's load-bearing |
|---|---|
| **No channel** | The largest. Distribution decides both market models and every hypothesis about it is untested ([D-044](../log/decision-log.md)); there's no search demand to fall back on, and the one channel with real access is closed to Phase 0 ([D-046](../log/decision-log.md)). A product nobody encounters fails identically to one nobody wants, and the two are easy to confuse afterwards. |
| **Nothing brings anyone back** | Every conventional retention lever is banned on purpose. Two unproven mechanisms remain: the work being pleasant ([D-045](../log/decision-log.md)) and a response arriving. The hypothesis is that **the response is the retention mechanism, not the product**. |
| **The mood delta is the weakest number we have** | A tap on a session the user chose to start, in a hand-run cohort, run by the person who wrote the missions. Selection, demand characteristics, and the mere fact of finishing all push it positive independently of the product. |
| **Reclaimed time is felt but not bought** | The time framing scores lower on willingness to pay than the portfolio framing, and higher on frequency and open space. Both are guesses. |
| **The first ship lands in silence** | The audience ladder is the mitigation and it's unproven. If guaranteed responses feel manufactured, they won't update anyone's self-concept. |
| **How big must the first response be** | There's a threshold below which evidence doesn't change how someone sees themselves, and nobody knows where it sits. |
| **Who it's actually for** | The sibling project's field notes found heavier low-value-content consumption tracks with *less* self-recognition of a problem — so the people who most need this may be least likely to seek it. |
| **Payer mismatch** | Users have time and no money; institutions have money and long cycles needing evidence that requires users first. |

## Work log

**1 August 2026 — Review pass, then a 40% cut.** D-042 through D-047, written against an external review.

D-042 replaces D-017's framing with the position in full: you are free to do whatever you want, and not free of the consequences. It also settles a tension carried without naming — a mirror showing only the good side looked like kindness and is actually the sharper rule, because the lost evening has already been felt.

The rest are corrections. D-043 fixes an ordering error defended twice: the ladder was sequenced by intimacy, so rung one asked someone who avoids being seen to hand their first bad thing to a friend — more exposing than the crew D-033 deferred for that exact reason. D-044 fills the distribution hole. D-045 supplies mechanisms for D-035. D-046 stops the concierge phase recruiting minors. D-047 splits operator, responder, and interviewer.

Then the documents were cut by 40%, the harder half of the same review to act on. What went was duplication: the chain and the laziness thesis had been restated in four places, the design document carried its own copy of the goals and of eleven rejected alternatives that all live here, requirements described the core loop a second time in mechanics form, and this log's work entries had become a place to re-argue what the rows already say. Four stale items fell out on the way — `consistency = active days / planned days` (a discipline metric that outlived the discipline), a "reach their own definition of done" threshold D-028 had already deleted, "hand-picked crews" in Step 0 after D-033 deferred crews, and a `lib/scoring/` folder in a product that forbids scoring.
